Three takeaways

What this means for brands.

A complement, not a replacement

AI is used for specific tasks. Social media still led discovery (15 of 19), so AI adds a narrow, high-intent route.

Visibility follows relevance

Brands surfaced only when the prompt matched their category. Knowing a brand is not the same as recommending it.

Trust needs evidence

Consumers asked for real reviews, specifications and source links. Brands fear stale data and lost brand voice.
